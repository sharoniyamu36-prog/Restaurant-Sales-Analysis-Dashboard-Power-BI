# Restaurant-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI
An analysis of restaurant sales, revenue, profitability, and payment performance using Power BI.

## Project Overview

This project explores restaurant sales data using Power BI to understand revenue performance, profitability, product category performance, branch performance, and payment outcomes.

The aim was to turn raw sales data into a dashboard that makes it easier to monitor business performance, identify trends, and support better decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focused on answering these questions:

- How much revenue and profit did the restaurant generate?
- Which branches generated the highest net revenue?
- Which product categories contributed most to revenue?
- What proportion of payment records were successful, refunded, or failed?
- How did revenue and profit change across quarters?
- Which areas of the business may require further investigation?

## Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation, where applicable.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and key performance indicators.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation

I reviewed the available dataset and prepared the relevant fields for analysis. This stage focused on ensuring that the data could support the required calculations and visualizations.

### 2. KPI Development

I identified key performance indicators to monitor the restaurant's financial and operational performance:

- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Refunded Payment Value
- Net Revenue
- Net Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Products

### 3. Data Analysis and Visualization

I built a Power BI dashboard containing the following visuals:

- Net Revenue by Branch
- Net Revenue by Product Category
- Payment Status Distribution
- Net Revenue and Net Profit by Quarter
- Total Revenue by Customer
- KPI cards for the main performance indicators
- A date slicer for interactive analysis

## Key Findings

The dashboard highlighted several areas worth investigating:

**1. Revenue and Profitability**

The dashboard reports approximately ₦41.22M in total revenue, ₦39.79M in net revenue, and ₦16.09M in net profit. These measures provide a starting point for evaluating the restaurant's financial performance.

**2. Product Category Performance**

Protein generated approximately ₦9.97M in net revenue, making it the leading category among those displayed. Rice followed with approximately ₦6.48M.

**3. Branch Performance**

Benin - Ring Road recorded the highest net revenue among the branches displayed, at approximately ₦5.9M.

**4. Payment Outcomes**

Successful payments accounted for approximately 93.53% of payment records. Refunded and failed transactions represent areas for further investigation.

**5. Quarterly Performance**

Revenue and profit peaked in Q3 before declining in Q4. Further analysis would be needed to determine whether this pattern reflects seasonality, changes in customer demand, or operational factors.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the initial findings, I would recommend that the restaurant:

- Monitor demand and stock availability for high-performing product categories.
- Investigate differences in branch performance to identify practices that could be adopted elsewhere.
- Review refund and failed-payment records to identify recurring issues and opportunities to reduce revenue leakage.
- Examine monthly sales trends and promotional activities to understand the Q3 peak and Q4 decline.
- Compare profit margins and operating costs across branches and product categories before making expansion or inventory decisions.

These recommendations should be validated through further analysis before operational changes are made.
